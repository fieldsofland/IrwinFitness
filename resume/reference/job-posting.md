## About Air
Air is a Creative Ops System for creative teams. The product automates the
mindless tasks creatives and marketers do every day to manage content, and
unlocks creativity through image recognition, automated versioning, and approval
workflows. Launched March 2021; raised $70M+ from Avenir, Tiger Global, Headline
Ventures, Lerer Hippeau, WndrCo, and Slack Ventures.

## The Role
Air is hiring a Senior Writer to own its voice across LinkedIn and Twitter/X.
This hire makes Air and its CEO unavoidable online: building a recognizable brand
voice, shaping how the market perceives Air beyond "DAM software," and
ghostwriting high-performing content that compounds over time. Requires deep
understanding of software, internet culture, and how positioning wins attention.

## Core Responsibilities
- Write daily content across LinkedIn and Twitter/X for both the Air brand and CEO accounts
- Develop recurring content formats, narratives, and POVs that build long-term audience equity
- Turn product launches, customer stories, internet moments, and internal thinking into high-performing posts
- Ghostwrite posts, threads, launch copy, and reactive commentary for leadership
- Work closely with leadership to sharpen messaging and positioning
- Study competitors, creative tools, AI startups, and software trends obsessively
- Identify opportunities for Air to participate in conversations before they go mainstream
- Maintain a fast feedback loop around what works and what doesn't; experiment constantly with tone, format, visuals, and distribution

## Requirements
